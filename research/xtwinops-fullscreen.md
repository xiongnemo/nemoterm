# XTWINOPS fullscreen on Windows

`CSI 10 ; 1 t` asks to enter fullscreen, `0` leaves and `2` toggles. An omitted second parameter restores the window. The argument dispatch follows `ewFullscreenWin` in the [pinned XTerm 412 source](https://github.com/ThomasDickey/xterm-snapshots/blob/5c75ead9a75d4b2e666ca06fa94503b5766ed93f/charproc.c); see also [XTerm Control Sequences](https://invisible-island.net/xterm/ctlseqs/ctlseqs.html). This is a source comparison; native Windows monitoring independently measures what the product does with the bytes.

On product `3da2fc3`, both host types ignored the enter request. On the hosted 1024×768 monitor the standalone window stayed at `[130,130,829,711]`; the tab host stayed at `[26,26,725,635]`. Both retained their caption and sizing border. The [original archive](xtwinops-fullscreen-before-3da2fc3.zip) from [run 44400868844](https://github.com/xiongnemo/nemoterm/actions/runs/44400868844) has SHA-256 `87676d4046f50b3307e42100fc2b00fab0f6c2037b1dac70190302a9782e4053`. Use `identity.json` and the two `*window-resize-observed.json` files for this run. Additional historical receipts present in the unmodified archive do not describe this execution.

`d117282` routes fullscreen requests to the existing Windows fullscreen action. A resize-disabled profile ignores them. A background tab cannot change the host, and a disabled or minimized host does not act on the request. Acceptance checks enter from both a normal and a maximized window, leave to the original rectangle and frame, and exercise multiple toggles in one write. The two rejected-policy profiles must also retain their exact original state. The companion OSC capability reports only advertise `FullscreenWin` on frontends with this operation.
